I am a PhD Candidate in the [ECE Department](https://www.ece.cmu.edu/) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), advised by [Prof. James Hoe](http://users.ece.cmu.edu/~jhoe/doku/doku.php). My research is in **reconfigurable computing at datacenter scale**, as part of the [Crossroads FPGA Center](https://www.crossroadsfpga.org). I am also affiliated with [CALCM](https://research.ece.cmu.edu/~calcm/doku.php).

My current research investigates the design and optimization of input-dependent streaming pipelines on FPGAs, developing abstractions to model and optimize applications across networking, security, and database analytics workloads.
