RapidQ introduces a novel queuing abstraction for performance modeling of input-dependent streaming pipelines on FPGAs. We devise a modeling framework to extract the model directly from HLS implementations with >97% accuracy. Our queueing-based RapidQ simulator in SystemC achieves a >7x speedup over state-of-the-art tools, enabling an automated tuning flow that reduces resource utilization by over 40% across synthetic and real-world network security and machine learning workloads.
