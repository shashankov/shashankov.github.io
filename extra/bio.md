# About

Hey, this is Shashank. Let's get to know me better! I am a student in the [Electrical and Computer Engineering Department](https://www.ece.cmu.edu) at [Carnegie Mellon Univeristy](https://www.cmu.edu). I completed my Bachelor's and Master's degree in [Electrical Engineering](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in) from the [Indian Institute of Technology Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in), India in June of 2019. My research interests include High-Performance Computing, Computer Architecture and Digital Design especially, involving reconfigurable architectures and SoC Design. I am also interested in exploring RF and Mixed-Signal design. Apart from Electrical Engineering, Genetics and Molecular Biochemistry also interest me, which motivated me to pursue Minor in the Biosciences and Bioengineering Department at my undergraduate university.

As a part of my Master's Thesis, I worked on the project of accelerating Harmonic Balance for RF circuits, with [Prof. Sachin Patkar](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/wiki/faculty/patkar) which involves accelerating periodic steady-state simulation, with the end goal being an implementation on a host-accelerator system with the accelerator being an FPGA or GPU. I also worked on other projects which involve circuit partitioning, using graph algorithms and sub-modular functions, which are being increasingly used in machine learning.

I also worked with [Prof. Maryam Baghini](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/wiki/faculty/mshojaei) on an Oscillator design project for a MEMS-based Particulate Measurement System and did [Teaching Assistantships](../teaching.md) under [Prof Rajesh Zele](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~rajeshzele) for his courses CMOS Analog and RF IC Design.

After my third year at IIT Bombay, I did an internship at the University of Tokyo with [Prof. Reiji Suda](http://olab.is.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~reiji/) in his HPC Lab, where I worked with three major domains in numerical computation: GPU acceleration and coding using CUDA, multi-processor programming using openMP, and distributed computing using MPI. In my second year, I worked with the [IIT Bombay Racing](https://www.iitbracing.org/) team in the Data Acquisition sub-division within the Electrical division. The team designs electric racecars to participate in the Formula Student competition and our subdivision and was involved in developing the first CAN network for the team, and implementing the wireless telemetry.

On a lighter note, in my spare time I enjoy watching TV Series (Star Trek, F.R.I.E.N.D.S., The Big Bang Theory, and Doctor Who are few of my favorites), reading fiction (Sherlock Holmes, Harry Potter, Bartimaeus Trilogy...) and most importantly [Pokémon](https://www.pokemon.com): I own a proud collection of all the RPG games (at least one of each region) with Rayquaza[^2] being my favorite Pokémon! Also, as I really enjoyed my visit to Japan and their culture, I learned Japanese during my last two years at IIT Bombay.
